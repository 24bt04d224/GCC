# GCC Campaign Hub - Quick Start Guide

Welcome to the GCC Campaign Hub! Send personalized WhatsApp and Email messages directly from your browser.

---

### Step 1: Upload Your Data
1. Go to the **Data Hub** tab.
2. Drag & Drop your Excel (.xlsx) file.
3. Check the "Column Mapping" section to make sure the Name, Phone, and Email columns are matched correctly.

### Step 2: Write Your Message
1. Go to the **Dispatcher** tab.
2. Type your message in the Template box on the left.
3. Click the blue buttons (like `+Name`) to insert variables. 
   *(Example: "Hi {{Name}}" will automatically change to "Hi John" when you send it).*

### Step 3: Send!
1. On the right side, you will see a card for every person on your list.
2. Click **WhatsApp** to open WhatsApp with the message already typed for you. Hit send!
3. Click **Email** to open Gmail with the To, Subject, and Body already filled out. Hit send!
4. The card will automatically turn green to show you already contacted them.

---
**⚠️ Quick Tips:**
- Do not send WhatsApp messages too fast to avoid getting your account blocked. Take breaks!
- If you refresh the page, your data is saved. If you clear your browser history, your data will be erased.
- Make sure your browser allows Pop-ups for this site.
